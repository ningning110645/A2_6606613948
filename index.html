Reflection♥

What I did: I took on the role of a staff member at the Varnasin Volunteer Camp, where I facilitated group activities for 50+ participants. All of them were elementary school students. I read the English books to them and helped them with their reading, while also engaging in the activity and encouraging them to speak English.

What I learned: I learned that active listening and empathy are important soft skills in this generation. As the participants are elementary school kids, they need a lot of understanding and patience.

Challenge: The main challenge was understanding how to talk and engage with the kids, make sure they felt comfortable with the activities, and encourage them to participate and speak English. Therefore, I focused on building rapport with the children by playing games with them and giving them some snacks and presents.

Next step: I plan to develop my communication skills further and I will continue to practice my active listening skill as well, because these soft skills are necessary in today's world where we need to understand diversity and contribute positively to society.
